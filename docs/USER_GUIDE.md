# PocketVR user guide

PocketVR plays SteamVR games on your Android phone. Game audio stays on your PC.

## Set up

1. Install SteamVR from Steam.
2. Run `PocketVR-0.1.0-x64-setup.exe`. Windows may warn about an unknown publisher: choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**. Approve the firewall prompt.
3. Open **PocketVR** from your desktop.
4. On your phone, turn on USB debugging once:
   1. Open **Settings**, then **About phone**, and tap **Build number** seven times.
   2. Go back to **Settings**, open **System**, then **Developer options**, and turn on **USB debugging**.
5. Plug your phone in with a USB cable and tap **Allow** on the phone.
6. In PocketVR on your PC, open **Phone** and click **Install on phone**.

## Play

1. Click **Start SteamVR** in PocketVR.
2. Open PocketVR on your phone and tap **Connect**. There is nothing to pair.
3. Tap **Enter VR**, put the phone in your headset, and start a game.

USB is used automatically when the cable is plugged in. Otherwise PocketVR uses Wi-Fi, so keep both devices on the same network, ideally 5 GHz.

## Lenses

On your phone, open **Settings**. Under **Headset**, tap **Scan headset QR code**, or **Adjust lenses by hand** if your headset has no code.

Under **Display**, choose **No headset (single view)** to play without a headset.

## If something goes wrong

| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| The phone can't reach the PC | Make sure SteamVR is running. Then open **Help** on the PC and click **Repair firewall**. |
| The picture stays black | Open **Help** on the PC and click **Restart SteamVR**. |
| The phone says it's a different PC | On the phone, open **Settings** and tap **Forget this PC**. |
| The view drifts or points the wrong way | Press **Ctrl+Alt+R** on the PC keyboard, or **Back** on an Xbox pad. You can also click **Recenter view** on the PC, tap **Recenter** on the phone, or double tap the screen in VR. |
| The picture looks blocky | On the PC, open **Settings** and choose **Sharp**. On USB, raise **Highest bitrate** under **Advanced settings**. |

To ask for help, open **Help** on the PC and click **Save a support file**.

## Good to know

Without a pairing step, any device on your network with the PocketVR app can connect to your PC while SteamVR is running. Use it on a home network you trust.

For games that are not on SteamVR, Revive can run Oculus titles. It is a separate download.
