Phone

Install the phone app

Plug in your phone with a USB cable. The first time, turn on USB debugging on the phone.

  1. Open Settings, then About phone.
  2. Tap Build number seven times.
  3. Go back to Settings, open System, then Developer options.
  4. Turn on USB debugging, then tap Allow when the phone asks.

Connect by QR code

If the phone can't find this PC, open Settings on the phone and tap Scan the code on your PC.
